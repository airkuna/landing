Product · money

mpt.hr — zero fees end to end, with a test suite that enforces it.

Card in → on-chain euro → bank account out, and the user pays 0 € at every step. The interesting part isn't the claim; it's that the claim is asserted in code on every test run.

  • ✓
    Invariant tests, not marketing copy. npm test asserts three things: the user pays 0 € at each step, amounts are preserved 1:1, and no balance is ever negative.
  • ↔
    The rail is in production on pay.domovina.ai — a real IBAN, real euro settlement on Gnosis Chain, seconds rather than days.
  • ⌘
    Self-custody by default. A passkey instead of a seed phrase; the user's keys stay with the user, so we are never in the path of the money.

Product · identity

Proof of Personhood: a state eID login that becomes an on-chain identity.

Log in with the national eID → the backend derives an irreversible nullifier from the national ID number → a soulbound token is minted on-chain. One person, any number of wallets, one vote. The raw national ID never touches the chain. Croatia's Certilia today; the EU's EUDI Wallet is the same shape tomorrow, for all 27 states.

The contracts exist

IdentityRegistry + PersonhoodSBT (EIP-5484) + an EIP-712 verifier, in Solidity. 55 Foundry tests green, with a deploy script.

The verifier runs end to end

The off-chain harness is proven e2e against a local EVM: a duplicate national ID reverts, and migrateAnchor moves an existing identity to a new wallet.

Written down properly

Whitepaper v0.2 plus an architecture-decision-record base, public at airkuna.com/en/whitepaper/.

not shipped None of this is on a mainnet, and I'm not claiming it is. Missing: the mainnet deploy, the verifier against the real eID endpoint, a security audit, and the signer network. Even the testnet deploy is stuck — the faucet wouldn't fund the deployer wallet. What exists today is code, tests and a local harness, which is the easy half of the work.

Sources: github.com/airkuna/landing — contracts/ and services/verifier-a2/. Test count from the Foundry suite, 30 July 2026.

The slide most decks don't have

What's genuinely hard, and what's still open.

You've both seen enough decks to know the shape of one that only points in a single direction. Here's the other direction, at the same level of detail as the good news.

The fee wasn't always 15%

It was 0% until 7 July, then 25% until 12 July, then 15%. An admin can change it with one call. I published my own earlier, wrong figure rather than quietly correcting it — but the underlying risk is real, and it isn't mine to control.

The protocol isn't mine

It's a third party, in production but still in closed testing. Small TVL, and the top three wallets hold ≈72% of it. I'm a user, not the author, and if it fails I lose the position like anyone else.

Personhood is code, not a network

No mainnet, no audit, no signer network, no verifier against the real eID endpoint, and a testnet deploy blocked on a faucet. Contracts and tests are the easy half.

The stablecoin is a concept

airKUNA is not an issued or licensed stablecoin. There's a design, a whitepaper and a DAO in formation. Anyone describing it as issued is ahead of the facts.

One person on the critical path

Almost all of the above shipped solo. That's why it exists at this cost, and it's also the single biggest risk to any of it continuing.

Funding isn't closed

Stated as a fact, not as an ask — there is deliberately no ask for money in this deck. It's simply the honest answer to the obvious question about how long this pace holds.
